Allergo Tracker helps capture ingestion, exposure, and outbreak events on-device
without mandatory Play, Firebase, billing, or other proprietary mobile-service
dependencies.

The app preserves explicit export confirmation, platform-scoped import access,
and a minimal permission surface so alternative distribution remains practical
without weakening privacy boundaries.

The current publication package is prepared for F-Droid review, with the
remaining publication blocker documented explicitly: provide the production
signing certificate fingerprint for AllowedAPKSigningKeys.
